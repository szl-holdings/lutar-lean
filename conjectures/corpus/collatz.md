# Collatz conjecture (prior art)

The Collatz conjecture concerns the map C(n) = n/2 for even n and C(n) = 3n + 1
for odd n. It asserts that for every positive integer n, repeated application of
C eventually reaches 1. Also known as the 3n+1 problem, the Syracuse problem, or
the Ulam conjecture. It remains unproven.
